To your DAW

Audio out

Channels 1–2 the mix, 3 the star, 4 planets 1–3, 5 planets 4–6, 6 the drums, 7 the weather and the radio, 8 the echo and the reverb. The voices come dry, at the mix's level. It needs an output with 8 channels: an audio interface, or a loopback driver such as BlackHole (Mac) or VB-Audio (Windows) set as the browser's output, recorded into the DAW.

MIDI

MIDI out sends what the MIDI out page asks for (press GLO three times): each planet's notes on its own channel (1–6 by default), the drums on 7 (notes 36–41, a lane each), and the system's signals as CCs on 8: 20–25 each planet's tide, 26 the star's roar, 27 the dust, 28 the Great Year, 29 a flare. Clock from a DAW: Sync on the Setup page (Clock by default); START plays from step one, STOP rests it, and the steps and the orbits follow the clock. To reach a DAW on this computer, use a virtual MIDI port: the IAC Driver (Mac) or loopMIDI (Windows).

Pages and knobs

One page a button; SELECT walks them all, each planet's page in turn, and the dots at the right of the knob strip show where you are. Each system (PRESETS) remembers its own settings.

System HOME

Planets
One to six; the rest go quiet (their lanes still drum).
Tempo
30–90 BPM (also HOME + SELECT). A year is 32 bars over a lane's hits.
Wander
How much a planet's phrase changes each year.
Phrase
How many notes a planet plays round its orbit: Auto (more for the slow ones) or 3–8.
Orbit view
A minute untouched here: the orbits fill the screen and the lights play along (each planet a page button and its note's key, the drums black keys 1–6, OCT± the beats). Any touch brings the screen back.

Star ENV

Level
The star's loudness, and the ceiling its roar and flares stay under.
Colour
Its noise, and its colour: White, Pink, Brown (a brown dwarf), Blue (a blue giant), Violet, Grey, Black (a black hole, crackling), Yellow (radio hiss).
Roar, Flares
How often it builds into a low roar; how often it flares.

Planet ARP · ALGORITHM / SEL picks which

Level
Its loudness, and its size: bigger planets swell smaller ones as they pass.
Instrument
Sixteen: Drone, Glass bell, Pluck, Soft keys, Choir, Laser, Ether, Ring hiss, and the dark ones: Abyss, Dark choir, Cello, Gong, Toll, Whisper, Cold pad, Dusk organ.
Ellipse, Octave
A circle to a long ellipse: it warms and cools on every orbit. Two octaves down or up (also OCT− / OCT+).

Gravity LFO

Tide, Reach
How much a passing neighbour swells a planet, and from how far.
Wake
How loud a dormant planet sounds when a tide wakes it.
Great year
All: the outermost planet's year, dormant ones too. Active: only those that play.

Weather LFO twice

Dust
How often a dust cloud comes. A Mellow closes over the whole mix as it thickens, warmer, softer and grainy, then opens out again as it thins.
Asteroids
How often a stray rock crosses, swelling the planets it passes, rumbling.
Comets
How often one falls into the star, ringing bells, and burns up.
Events
How loud the rumble and the bells are. Black keys 1–3 call each now.

Pulse SEQ

Lane
Kick, Pulsar, Sonar, Geiger, Rim, Shaker; lane n is planet n's. The white keys are its 16 steps, black keys 1–6 pick it. Hold SEQ for the sequencer: the whole bank on screen, SELECT one of eight banks (it plays from the next bar), OCT− / OCT+ or black keys the lane.
Binding
What each hit does to the planet: Off, Pump, Blip, Flicker, Nudge.
Drums, Evolve
Their level; at each Great Year Shift moves a hit or two, Drift also adds or takes one (and the planets move to new orbits).

Space FX

Echo, Reverb
A dotted-eighth echo; the plate (more, longer). The far planets send most.
Radio, Width
A band of hiss and the odd falling whistler; how wide the planets spread.

Setup GLO

Root, Mode
The key; a scale for the keys to start from.
Tune, Sync
Fine tuning in cents; Free, or Clock: follows MIDI clock while it comes (EXT and the tempo top right). SAVE here saves all ten systems.

Display GLO twice

Theme
Deep space, Nebula, Aurora, Red planet, Phosphor, Redtail, Mono. The planets keep their warm-to-cold colours and the star its own.
Starfield, Orbits, Tide lines
How bright each is; 0 hides it.

MIDI out GLO three times

Sends, Channels
Off, Notes, Notes+CC or CC; which eight channels (1–8 to 9–16).
Lights, CPU
The key lights; the audio load.

Buttons

  • Keys are the scale: each one adds or removes its note. The root stays. On Pulse they are the steps, on Weather black keys 1–3 call the weather.
  • PLAY grows / rests. EDIT the Great Year now: the chord moves, the drums evolve. REC eclipse: the orbits and the weather hold, the space rings on.
  • SELECT every page in turn; with HOME held, the tempo. ALGORITHM and SEL the planet (on Pulse, the lane), PRESETS the system.
  • OCT− / OCT+ the selected planet's octave. SAVE on the Setup pages saves; elsewhere it starts the system again; hold it to put the system back to its recipe.
  • ARP flashes as the selected planet plays. Hold a button for a second and a card says what it does (with a mouse: Shift-click to hold it).

Mouse, touch, keyboard

  • Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it. Click or touch buttons and keys.
  • White keys 1–8: Q–I, 9–16: A–K. Black keys: 1–0, -.
  • Space PLAY, Enter REC, M the Great Year (EDIT), Esc HOME, Z / X OCT− / OCT+, ↑ ↓ SELECT.

The systems

Ten, one for each of the star's colours and two more: Sol (yellow), Lumen (white), Rosette (pink), Ember (a brown dwarf, dusty, an abyss under it), Rigel (a blue giant), Veil (violet), Cinder (grey), Horizon (a black hole: the darkest and slowest, every voice a dark one, a quiet drifting planet), Kuiper (cold, slow, full of dormant worlds and comets) and Nova (quick, bright, flaring).

What you hear is the firmware running at the FM-1's 44.1 kHz, not a recording. Running FM-1 firmware in the browser comes from Groove OS's emulator, by way of AMB-1.
