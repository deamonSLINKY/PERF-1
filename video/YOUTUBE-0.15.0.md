## Description (paste as is; the chapter lines make YouTube's chapters)

PERF-1 0.15.0 is out: free, open-source live-performance firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1 (GPL-3.0). One take of one piece, synthwave 1, from switching the unit on to the credits, made from the firmware itself in its browser emulator: what you hear is what the FM-1 plays.

Install (Chrome or Edge, the FM-1 on USB): https://deamonslinky.github.io/PERF-1/install/
Manual, browser emulator, editor: https://deamonslinky.github.io/PERF-1/
Source and release notes: https://github.com/deamonSLINKY/PERF-1/releases/tag/v0.15.0

New in 0.15.0: each genre changes chord every so many bars, so the progressions are heard (lofi's ii-V-I, house's i-VII-VI-VII, synthwave's i-VI-III-VII), with a real dominant in minor keys and chord loops that start from home. The saw pads sweep a resonant filter with every breath and stab. Nine named bass figures. The pluck on MIDI channel 2 and a Pluck steps page that freezes its loop as sixteen steps you write. A SET page with rounds, a tempo ramp and an intro that brings the tracks in one by one for mixing in. The Synth theme as a look of its own. The DY voices, human-made DX7 voices from public-domain banks, by default. Thank you to everyone who installed 0.14.0 and sent ideas: most of this update is yours. Keep them coming: issues on GitHub, the Firmware Hub, the comments.

PERF-1 is a fork of HYP-1 (RedtailWorks) and AMB-1 (Charles Vestal) on the Felucca platform layer (Leo Kuroshita); the FM engine and DX7 voices come from Melodee (Kerem Kilic), after Dexed and MSFA; generator ideas from Tuna (delirioux). Thirty DY voices from public-domain DX7 banks (Bobby Blues' page, Dave Benson's page). Installing firmware is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back and backs up what the unit stores.
