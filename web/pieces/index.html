Pieces

Share a piece you saved on your FM-1, or your whole set list, as a file, a code or a link, and add what other people share. Everything in a piece goes with it: its settings, its drum patterns and step tricks, a frozen acid or pluck line, your chord progression, and the DX7 voice its pluck plays if it is one you loaded yourself. Find pieces to try in the Library.

Plug the FM-1 in by USB and use Chrome or Edge. Adding needs PERF-1 0.16.3 or later; sharing works from any version that has Backup.

Adding writes into the FM-1 and restarts it (a few seconds). Save what you are playing first: changes you have not saved are lost in the restart.

Share from your FM-1

Save the piece on the FM-1 first (GLO, then SAVE): only saved pieces can be shared. The first row shares your set list: every piece in it (stored or factory), their voices, Play and the Mix.

Post it with the link: the picture shows what it is, the link adds it.

Post your pieces on Reddit, under a video, or suggest one for the Library: a link is enough.
