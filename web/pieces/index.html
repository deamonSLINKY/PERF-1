Library & pieces

Library My FM-1 Add a code

Pieces and sets made by PERF-1 players. Hear one in the browser emulator or add it to your FM-1 (it asks before it replaces anything of yours); a piece brings its own DX7 voice. PICKED ones are the maker's choice. Share yours from My FM-1: tick them and press Submit.

Plug the FM-1 in by USB (Chrome or Edge). Only saved pieces are here: save one on the FM-1 with GLO, then SAVE. The first row is your set list: every piece in it, their voices, Play and the Mix.

Several ticked: give each a title if you like (left empty, it goes by its genre and number); your name goes on all of them. Tick one alone to add a note.

Post it with the link: the picture shows what it is, the link adds it.

Submitting puts them in the Library for everyone at once, with your name if you give one. You can take back what you submitted from this browser (Remove, on its card). Anything else, a link is enough: post it on Reddit or under a video.

Adding writes into the FM-1 and restarts it (a few seconds). Save what you are playing first: changes you have not saved are lost in the restart. Adding needs PERF-1 0.16.3 or later.
