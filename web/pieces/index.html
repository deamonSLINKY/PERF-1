Pieces

Share a piece you saved on your FM-1 as a file, a code or a link, and add the pieces other people share. Everything in the piece goes with it: its settings, its drum patterns and step tricks, a frozen acid or pluck line, your chord progression, and the DX7 voice its pluck plays if it is one you loaded yourself.

Plug the FM-1 in by USB and use Chrome or Edge. Both of you need PERF-1 0.16.3 or later to add a piece; sharing works from any version that has Backup.

Add a shared piece

or choose a .piece file:

Adding writes it into the FM-1 and restarts it (a few seconds). Save what you are playing first: changes you have not saved are lost in the restart.

Share a piece from your FM-1

Save the piece on the FM-1 first (GLO, then SAVE): only saved pieces can be shared. Then read them here.

Post your pieces in the PERF-1 discussions on GitHub, on Reddit or under a video: a link is enough.
