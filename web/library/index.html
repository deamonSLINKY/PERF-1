Library

Pieces and sets made by PERF-1 players. Hear one in the browser emulator, or add it to your FM-1 (it opens on the Pieces page, which asks before it replaces anything of yours). A piece brings its own DX7 voice with it.

Made something good? Share it from the Pieces page and press suggest one for the Library there, or open an issue with its link.
