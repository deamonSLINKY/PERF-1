To your DAW

Audio out

Channels 1–2 the mix, 3 lead, 4 comp, 5 bass, 6 answer, 7 drums, 8 dust. The voices come dry, at the mix's level. It needs an output with 8 channels: an audio interface, or a loopback driver such as BlackHole (Mac) or VB-Audio (Windows) set as the browser's output, recorded into the DAW.

MIDI

Remote: with LOF-1 on your FM-1 and MIDI connected here, hold SEL and press GLO on the FM-1. Its panel plays this emulator and goes quiet itself.

MIDI in: notes play the lead (from any keyboard you connect), and with Global › MIDI set to Clock or Both, a DAW's clock drives the tempo (START plays from the top, STOP stops). MIDI out (Global › MIDI: Notes or Both): the lead, comp, bass and answer on channels 1–4, the drums on 10. To reach a DAW on this computer, use a virtual MIDI port: the IAC Driver (Mac) or loopMIDI (Windows).

Pages and knobs

One button a voice; press it again for its second page. SELECT steps through all of them. ALGORITHM picks the page's instrument (or the kit, or DUST's character). PRESETS picks a preset, 01–10. Each keeps your changes while you play; SAVE on the Global page stores the one playing.

Range, Legato (short to tied notes), Repeat (how often it comes back to its last motif), Dynamics (how much it accents).

Buttons

  • Keys play the lead. The band hears which chord you imply and follows on the beat; stop playing and it carries on by itself.
  • PLAY play / stop. REC holds the chord, so you can solo over it. SAVE is Side B: a breakdown on the next bar; again, back in with a fill. On the Global page, SAVE stores the preset.
  • FX turns the keys into effects, and the screen shows a map of which key does what: stutters, record skip, half time, tape stop, backspin, muffle, radio, mutes, throws (white); wobble, crush, fill, grain freeze, dropout, solos (black). Hold SAVE in FX to lock effects on: tap as many keys as you like (they turn violet); keys already held lock too. Tap a locked key alone to turn it off. Press FX again and the locks keep playing while the keys play notes; SEL + FX turns every lock off.
  • SEL held + a key: the key's root. On the drum pages, SEL + white keys 1–8: the pattern. SEL + SEQ: a change on the next bar (as Drift's). SEL + HOME: the keys play single notes or whole chords. OCT− / OCT+ the lead's octave.
  • Hold a button for a second and a card says what it does (with a mouse: Shift-click to hold it).

Mouse, touch, keyboard

  • Drag a knob up or down, or use the scroll wheel over it. Click or touch buttons and keys.
  • Play like a DAW's keyboard: A is C4, W S E D F T G … up to ' (F5). Z X C V: F3, G3, A3, B3.
  • Space PLAY, Enter REC, Esc HOME, , / . OCT− / OCT+, ↑ ↓ SELECT. Or plug in a MIDI keyboard (Connect MIDI, then pick it under "MIDI in from").

The presets

Ten, numbered like HYP-1's. Each starts as one of these and becomes yours when you store over it:

  • 01 Rhodes, upright bass, vinyl. D Dorian, 78.
  • 02 The felt upright, vibes answering, a cassette. F major, 72.
  • 03 Muted trumpet, walking bass, Tape kit. G minor, 84.
  • 04 Kalimba over an organ, on reel to reel. C major, 88.
  • 05 Tenor sax, a warm pad, sub bass. A minor, 70.
  • 06 Strumstick arpeggios, harmonica, the radio. E Mixolydian, 90.
  • 07 Glock, tape strings, slow. B♭ major, 64.
  • 08 Hands off from the start (Daydream always). E minor pentatonic, 76.
  • 09 Wurli over organ offbeats, root-fifth bass, 9ths. A Dorian, 92.
  • 10 Late night: sine lead, pad, sub, a worn record, 11ths. E♭ minor, 60.

What you hear is the firmware running at the FM-1's 44.1 kHz, not a recording. The sampled instruments are Versilian Studios' CC0 libraries (VCSL, VSCO-2 CE). Running FM-1 firmware in the browser comes from Groove OS's emulator, by way of AMB-1 and ECO-1.
