The comments asked for more ways in and out, so the back edge now has them:

![The OPEN-1 port side: power, USB-C device and host, microSD, MIDI in and out with an A/B switch, sync in and out, CV 1 and 2, a pedal jack, audio in 1–2 and 3–4, audio out 1–2 and 3–4, and phones](media/open1-rear.jpg)

- **4 audio inputs and 4 outputs**, 24-bit, from a second audio chip. Plugged into a computer, OPEN-1 is also a 4 × 4 USB audio interface.
- **MIDI in and out** on 3.5 mm TRS, with an A/B switch so either cable standard works, plus MIDI over USB.
- **Sync in and out**, at whatever clock rate the firmware sets.
- **2 CV outputs** (0–5 V) for modular and older gear. With sync out as a gate, that's three CV/gate outputs.
- **A pedal jack** for a sustain or expression pedal.
- **USB-C host** for keyboards, controllers and thumb drives.

**One SD card holds everything:** firmware, samples and projects, in one folder per firmware. The firmwares you use most also sit in 16 MB of built-in storage, so OPEN-1 starts without a card. The chip also has a factory recovery mode in its ROM, so it can't be bricked: USB repair always works.

The extra jacks and the second audio chip add about $4 a unit, so the parts cost is now about $73, and the price targets don't change.
