Four firmwares into the M-VAVE FM-1, I keep running into the same walls: 1 MB of flash, about 432 KB of memory, no audio input and one firmware at a time. OPEN-1 is what I'd build to get past them.

It's an open, entry-level synth: 37 keys, pads, knobs, a bigger screen, and a microSD card full of firmware you can swap from the menu. The plan is to keep it under $200 and open it to firmware developers before it ships.

**What exists today:**

- A full target spec and parts list, with the parts priced at 1,000 units
- Drawings of the front panel and a block diagram, plus a first sketch of the circuit board
- A pitch deck for manufacturers and backers
- The concept video above, with OPEN-1 running a simulated HYP-1

**What doesn't exist yet:** hardware. Nothing here is for sale, and every spec and price is a target.

**Next:** a breadboard prototype on a Daisy Seed, which uses the same chip, so HYP-1 can run on the real processor.
