---
title: Two things I cut to keep the price down
date: 2026-10-17
tag: Design
---
**Bluetooth is out.** OPEN-1 connects by USB-C and MIDI cables only. Wired MIDI has lower and steadier latency, there's no radio to certify, and the money goes into the audio input and the MIDI output instead.

**The keys have no velocity.** They're silicone keys like the FM-1's, each lit by its own colour LED. That's cheaper and quicker to build than a velocity-sensing keybed. The 8 pads keep velocity, and a firmware can turn one of the spring levers into a velocity control if it wants one.

Both changes help OPEN-1 stay under $200 without giving up the things that make it more than an FM-1.
